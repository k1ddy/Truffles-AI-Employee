- Five Whys:
  1. Why did the turn fail: canon expected referent-followup evidence because the semantic envelope kept `subject_kind=service` and `resolution_mode=referent_followup`.
  2. Why was evidence missing: degraded collect returned from `policy_core_guard` before any referent resolver trace/meta was emitted.
  3. Why did degraded collect skip referent resolution: the shared resolver contract is only invoked in the `policy_valid and policy_tool_action` branch.
  4. Why is that inconsistent: even after validation failure, runtime still persists the semantic envelope in `decision_meta.llm_policy_core`, so the system claims a referent-followup interpretation without corresponding evidence.
  5. Why is this architectural and not phrase-specific: any degraded collect family that preserves `referent_followup` semantics can miss the same evidence, independent of the exact wording.

- Root cause statement:
  - semantic referent resolution is not wired into degraded collect branches, so invalid-but-preserved `referent_followup` plans can emit booking prompts without the `referent_resolver` trace/meta required by the contract.

- Fix mechanism:
  - backfill semantic referent resolution before degraded collect responses when the preserved policy-core envelope still requires or prefers a referent;
  - record `referent_resolver` trace and corresponding meta fields (`resolved_referent`, `referent_source`, `capability_resolution_mode`, `referent_resolution_decision`);
  - add deterministic regressions for the degraded-collect path and the canon guard.
